## Welcome to the Terje Sandstrøm Audio Technology site

For several years I worked on the design of audio equipment for very high
fidelity use, primarily for Electrocompaniet. As I no longer work actively in
that field, I've put as much of what I know onto this site for anyone to look
at.

This site covers the audio technology I worked on, the history of the early
Electrocompaniet period from 1975 to 1980 and their "Otala" 25W amplifier, plus
schematics, troubleshooting guides, theory articles, my M.Sc thesis on weak
non-linearities, and other audio-design work.

My current work is in software development — see
[my blog](https://hermit.no) for that side of things.
