# M.Sc Thesis — Weak Nonlinearities

**University of Oslo, 1985**

The thesis itself will be uploaded once it's converted, preferably to
English, and at least from printed form — it was written on a long-forgotten
computer and I'm no longer able to read the diskettes. (Update since the old
site: a scanned copy of the printed thesis exists and will be added here.)

Preceding my master thesis, I presented two papers at AES conventions in the
early '80s. One of these made it to the Journal of the AES — I've scanned it,
so take a look: it's about class AB distortion in power amplifiers. This
[article](/audio-design/ab-distortion/) was also used by the Danish High
Fidelity magazine, which published a follow-up article on it and presented an
amplifier modification of their own, based on this article. I've not been
able to locate that follow-up — I believe it was in 1983, possibly the
October issue. If anyone has a copy, I'd appreciate it :-)
